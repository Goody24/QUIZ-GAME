How to Play QuizVerse

⚡
Score & Streaks:

Each correct answer earns 10 base points. Chain consecutive correct answers for hot Streak Bonus Multipliers!

⏱️
Blitz Timer & Speed Bonus:

In Blitz Mode, answering within the first few seconds gives you up to +5 Speed Bonus Points.

💡
Lifelines:

Use the 50:50 lifeline to eliminate two incorrect choices, or +10s to freeze time.

⌨️
Keyboard Friendly:

Press 1, 2, 3, 4 or A, B, C, D on your keyboard to instantly select options, and Enter to proceed!
